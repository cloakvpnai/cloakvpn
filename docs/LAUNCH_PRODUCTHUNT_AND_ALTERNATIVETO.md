### First comment (the maker comment, post this immediately after launch)

Hi Product Hunt, I am the founder of Lattice VPN.

I built Lattice because of a problem most VPNs ignore: the encryption protecting your traffic today is not safe against quantum computers tomorrow. Worse, attackers can record encrypted traffic now and decrypt it years later once the hardware exists. It is called "harvest now, decrypt later," and anything with a long shelf life (health records, legal and financial data, your identity) is worth storing.

Lattice closes that window. Every connection pairs WireGuard, which is fast and modern, with a post-quantum key exchange (Rosenpass), so even a recorded session cannot be unlocked later.

A few decisions I care about:
- No account, no email, no name. Your only credential is an anonymous account number, so there is nothing personal to leak or hand over.
- Strict no-logs. No IP addresses, no DNS queries, no connection times.
- A kill switch and one-tap global servers.

It is live now on Google Play, with iOS in review. I would genuinely love your feedback, especially the hard questions about the cryptography and the no-logs model. I will be here all day to answer everything.

Thanks for taking a look.
